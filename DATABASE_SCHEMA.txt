Notes on the relationships
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- users.role_id -> roles: every account has one role (System Administrator, Parish Priest, Parish
  Staff, GKK Officer, PYA-KKK Officer, PPC Officer). GKK and PYA-KKK Officers share one portal
  (events, Chapels & Officers); PPC Officers approve their events. The "Parishioner" row remains
  only because a retired, deactivated account still points to it.
- community_events are set up by GKK and PYA-KKK Officers (created_by_user_id) and approved or
  rejected by a PPC Officer (reviewed_by_user_id, review_note). Editing an event sets it back to
  Pending.
- chapels and chapel_officers added by GKK and PYA-KKK Officers start with approval_status Pending
  (created_by_user_id) until a PPC Officer approves or rejects them (reviewed_by_user_id,
  review_note). Rows that existed before this rule are Approved. Officers can only be added to an
  Approved chapel; only Approved chapels are offered for public booking, and only Approved
  officers can be assigned to events.
- community_event_officers links events to the chapel officers assigned to them (many-to-many).
  Removing a chapel officer or deleting an event removes the link.
- record_requests.requested_by_user_id and appointments_and_events.requested_by_user_id are now
  always NULL for new rows: parishioners no longer have accounts. They send requests from the
  public site and track them with record_requests.request_number (RR-XXXX-XXXX) or
  appointments_and_events.reference_number (AP-XXXX-XXXX). Old rows may still point at a retired,
  deactivated parishioner account.
- documents.uploaded_by_user_id is NULL for files uploaded from the public site.
- parishioners is the parish register of people (managed by staff). parishioners.user_id is a
  leftover link to the old parishioner accounts.
- parishioners.father_parishioner_id / mother_parishioner_id point back to parishioners (family
  tree).
- sacramental_records.primary_parishioner_id is the person who received the sacrament;
  secondary_parishioner_id is the spouse on a marriage record.
- sacramental_records.source_request_id links a register entry to the New Registration request
  that created it.
- sacramental_record_details shares its key with sacramental_records (one-to-one, extra fields per
  sacrament type).
- audit_logs.user_id is restricted on delete: an account with audit history is deactivated, never
  deleted.
